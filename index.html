FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT & UI/UX DESIGN

Thoughtful design.
Purposeful development.

I'm Marc, a frontend developer and Information Technology student from Angeles City. I enjoy creating responsive, accessible websites where strong visual design and practical functionality come together.

What I enjoy most is turning an idea into something people can actually use — from early layouts and Figma prototypes to carefully built interfaces. I pay attention to the details that make a difference: typography, spacing, clarity, and the way each interaction feels.

My main focus is frontend development and UI/UX design. I also have experience working with backend technologies through my projects, which helps me understand how interfaces fit into a complete web application.
